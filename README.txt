Instructions for the program

After starting the program, user interface will load displaying four Northeastern University parking lots, each containing a predefined set of parking spots.
Each parking lot will show all available and occupied parking spots, along with the details for each of them.

The top left section of the screen is used for gathering the inputs on vehicle details, including vehicle type, power, plate number and state from the user.

Upon program initiation, five default vehicles are created and automatically parked in various parking lots.


Parking a vehicle:
In the top right section of the screen, users can select a parking lot in which they wish to park their vehicle. They can then enter vehicle details using drop down menus and text fields. 
Once the information is entered, clicking the Park button will initiate the parking process matching the vehicle attributes.
Please note that plate number should be 6 upper case letters or numbers, and state should be two upper case letters. Large size spot is required for Van and Truck; standard size or larger may be used for a car, small size or larger may be used for a motorcycle; gas powered vehicles can only park in gas compatible spots and electric vehicles can only park in EV spots.
Upon a series of validations, the Valet Parking Assistant will either successfully park the vehicle in a first compatible and available spot or display a message commenting why the vehicle cannot be parked.


Unparking a vehicle:
Locate and select your vehicle in one of the four parking lot lists displayed on the screen. Once the vehicle is selected and highlighted in blue, click Unpark button. The Valet Parking Assistant will display the message after successfully completing the action.

Clear the selections:
Click on Clear button to quickly reset previously entered selections in the top left section the screen.
